Admission Fee
Admission Fee of Rs 30,000 is payable once at the time of admission. If a graduate of this University is admitted to a higher degree program, then admission fee is not payable again.

Security Deposit
A refundable security deposit of Rs 20,000 is also payable at the time of admission.

Tuition Fee (w.e.f. Fall 2026 semester)
Tuition Fee is payable in full before the start of each semester. Annual revision will be applicable as per University Internal Rules. For the academic year 2026-27 the tuition fee per credit hour applicable to all batches is as follows:
Program: BBA / BS; Fee: Rs. 12,000
Program: MBA / MS; Fee: Rs. 12,000
Program: PhD; Fee: Rs. 12,000

Student Activities Fund
To support co-curricular and extra-curricular activities a Student Activities Fund has been formed. Students contribute Rs 2,500 towards Students Activities Fund per semester.

Fee Refund Policy
1. Admission Fee is non-refundable. 2. Security deposit is refundable at the time of completion of degree or closing of admission. 3. Tuition Fee is refunded in accordance with the guidelines of Higher Education Commission (HEC).
Timeline: Upto 10th day of commencement of classes; Percentage of Fee: 100% Fee Refund
Timeline: Upto 15th day of commencement of classes; Percentage of Fee: 80% Fee Refund
Timeline: Upto 20th day of commencement of classes; Percentage of Fee: 60% Fee Refund
Timeline: Upto 30th day of commencement of classes; Percentage of Fee: 50% Fee Refund
Timeline: 31st day onwards of commencement of classes; Percentage of Fee: No Refund
Note: Days shall be counted including working days and holidays.
After declaration of FSc/A Level/Equivalent result, admission of a student stands cancelled if he/she fails to meet the required eligibility requirement for admission. The fee refund is calculated from the date of his/her result announcement.
